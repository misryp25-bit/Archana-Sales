Our Story

From a small distribution setup in Raipur to one of Chhattisgarh's most trusted building materials dealers — the milestones that got us here.

Our Journey

Milestones

Fifteen years of steady growth, one brand authorisation and one satisfied customer at a time.

2008

Foundation & First Steps

Archana Sales was founded in Raipur with a vision to make genuine construction materials easily accessible to builders and contractors in Chhattisgarh. We began as a small dealership focused on quality over quantity.

2012

Expanding the Brand Portfolio

We secured authorised dealerships with Fosroc Chemicals and Asian Paints — two of India's most respected brands. This milestone marked our transition from a small trader to a multi-brand authorised dealer.

2017

New Office & Growth

We moved to our current office at Srishti Garden Commercial, Telibandha, and expanded our team. Adding JK Laxmi Cement and Jindal Panther Cement made us a comprehensive one-stop building material supplier.

2021

Industrial Paints Division

We took on Asian Paints PPG — the 50:50 joint venture of Asian Paints and PPG Industries, USA — adding protective, powder, floor and traffic coatings for plants, refineries and infrastructure work to our range.

Now

Trusted Across Chhattisgarh

Today Archana Sales is a five-brand authorised dealer with 500+ happy customers, serving major construction projects, housing developments and infrastructure works across all of Chhattisgarh. We are proud to be a name builders trust.
