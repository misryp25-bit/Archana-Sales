Who We Are

Archana Sales – Building Trust, One Project at a Time

Archana Sales is one of Chhattisgarh's most trusted authorised dealers and distributors of premium construction and building materials. Founded and headquartered in Raipur, we serve contractors, builders, architects, real estate developers and homeowners across the entire state.

We hold official dealership authorisations from some of India's most respected brands — Fosroc Chemicals Pvt. Ltd., Asian Paints Decorative & Asian Paints PPG, Nuvoco Cement, JK Laxmi Cement and Jindal Panther Cement — ensuring every product we supply is 100% genuine, directly sourced and backed by manufacturer warranties.

Our mission is simple: make high-quality building materials accessible, affordable and reliably available to every construction project in Chhattisgarh — from a single home to a large infrastructure development.
